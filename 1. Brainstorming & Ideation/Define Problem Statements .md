# Define Problem Statements

## 1. The Core Agricultural Challenge
Smallholder farmers globally face rising uncertainty due to climate change, declining soil fertility, and unpredictable rainfall. Traditional farming methods, which rely heavily on seasonal history and generational intuition, are no longer sufficient to guarantee high yields or protect crop health.

## 2. Problem Statement 1: Crop-Weather Mismatch
- **Description:** Farmers frequently plant crops that are ill-suited to the current season's temperature, humidity, and rainfall.
- **Consequence:** Crop failure, severe financial losses, increased rural debt, and food insecurity.
- **User Pain Point:** "I planted rice this year expecting heavy rains, but the monsoon was weak, and my crop failed because I didn't know maize would be a better alternative."

## 3. Problem Statement 2: Soil Nutrient Imbalance
- **Description:** Farmers apply generic N-P-K (Nitrogen, Phosphorous, Potassium) fertilizers without understanding their soil's actual nutrient composition.
- **Consequence:** Soil degradation, high fertilizer costs, environmental pollution, and reduced long-term yields.
- **User Pain Point:** "I buy the same fertilizer every season, but my harvest is shrinking. The local soil lab takes three weeks to send results, and I can't wait that long."

## 4. Problem Statement 3: Lack of Actionable Soil Advice
- **Description:** Even when soil tests are performed, reports contain raw values (e.g., pH 5.2) without translating them into practical corrective steps.
- **Consequence:** Farmers do not know how to amend their soil (e.g., applying lime to counter acidity).
- **User Pain Point:** "The soil report says my pH is 5.5, but I don't know what that means for my banana plantation."

## 5. Opti-Crop Solution Statement
Opti-Crop bridges the gap between raw data and actionable intelligence. By taking instant user inputs of N, P, K, pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall, it predicts the highest-yielding crop with 94% accuracy, evaluates suitability, and provides instant, step-by-step chemical and organic recommendations to correct soil imbalances.
