# Empathy Map

## User Persona: Ramesh Kumar
- **Role:** Smallholder Farmer (3.5 acres of land)
- **Location:** Maharashtra, India
- **Tech Literacy:** Medium (Uses WhatsApp, YouTube, and mobile payment apps)

---

## 1. What does Ramesh SAY?
- "I need to know which crop will give me the best price at the market."
- "Fertilizers are getting more expensive every year. I want to use only what is necessary."
- "The local extension officer rarely visits our village, so we have to make decisions ourselves."

## 2. What does Ramesh THINK?
- *Am I planting the right crop for this year's late monsoon?*
- *I hope I can pay back the agricultural loan I took for seeds.*
- *Are these modern digital tools accurate, or should I stick to what my father did?*

## 3. What does Ramesh DO?
- Asks neighboring farmers what they are planting before buying seeds.
- Buys standard N-P-K fertilizer from the local dealer and applies it manually.
- Checks weather updates on local television broadcasts.
- Inputs farm parameters into mobile tools if recommended by fellow farmers.

## 4. What does Ramesh FEEL?
- **Anxiety:** Fear of crop failure due to drought or pest infestation.
- **Confusion:** Overwhelmed by fluctuating market prices and conflicting advice.
- **Hope:** Excited about the possibility of doubling yields using modern, scientific agricultural insights.
- **Frustration:** Tired of waiting weeks for soil test results from government agricultural extension labs.
