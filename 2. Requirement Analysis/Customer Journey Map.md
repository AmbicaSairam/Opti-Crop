# Customer Journey Map

## Persona: Ramesh Kumar (Farmer)

---

### Phase 1: Pre-Planting Planning
- **Goal:** Determine what crop to sow for the upcoming season.
- **Actions:** Ramesh checks his budget, estimates the monsoon arrival, and visits the local seed distributor.
- **Pain Points:** High uncertainty about weather patterns and soil health. Conflicting suggestions from neighbors.
- **Opportunity for Opti-Crop:** Raise awareness about digital soil-based crop prediction tools.

### Phase 2: Soil Testing & Data Input
- **Goal:** Input soil nutrients and weather conditions into Opti-Crop.
- **Actions:** Ramesh registers on the Opti-Crop app, inputs the values from a recent soil test kit (Nitrogen, Phosphorous, Potassium, pH), and inputs current weather estimates.
- **Pain Points:** Entering numeric decimal values can be tedious. App language might be difficult to understand.
- **Opportunity for Opti-Crop:** Provide clean, large input fields with clear units and simple placeholder text.

### Phase 3: Receiving Recommendations
- **Goal:** Get a reliable crop recommendation and advice.
- **Actions:** Ramesh clicks "Predict" and instantly receives the recommended crop (e.g., Chickpea) along with specialized watering and fertilizer tips.
- **Pain Points:** Unclear reasoning for the recommendation might breed skepticism.
- **Opportunity for Opti-Crop:** Explain *why* the crop was recommended by showing suitability stats and deviation details.

### Phase 4: Implementation & Action
- **Goal:** Purchase seeds and fertilize soil based on recommendations.
- **Actions:** Ramesh follows the advice (e.g., adding agricultural lime to raise soil pH) and purchases chickpea seeds.
- **Pain Points:** Finding recommended soil amendments in local markets.
- **Opportunity for Opti-Crop:** Suggest organic alternatives that are easily accessible locally.

### Phase 5: Post-Harvest Feedback
- **Goal:** Evaluate success and log outcomes.
- **Actions:** Ramesh harvests a successful crop with 20% higher yield, logs back in to view his prediction history, and plans for the next season.
- **Pain Points:** Forgetting previous parameter inputs.
- **Opportunity for Opti-Crop:** Store search history securely in the user dashboard.
